What I think (not what I measured)

Music is something that unites people.
It can draw a room together.
So many different minds occupied by different thoughts,
but they can all focus on a single song.

What has become of music over time?
What is being fed into people’s ears?

I have a premonition that there has been a deterioration in music over the decades.
So I took about six thousand Year-End songs as the object of this study.
And I recorded what people on the internet thought about music then vs. now.
Let’s see how their claims stand.

How this was done

Each song’s full lyric was read in context against the same fixed questions: does it describe sex graphically, is violence threatened or carried out, is drug use or dealing described (not just a drug named), and so on. Strong swearing is a word count from a fixed list, not a reading. Genre tags come from catalog listings and overlap: a song can be pop and hip-hop at once. “Without hip-hop” means every song with no hip-hop tag at all.

Every share is the songs that did the thing, out of all the songs in that period. Charts move in five-year steps, with 2025 folded into 2020–25. A genre is not drawn in a period where it has too few songs to count.
